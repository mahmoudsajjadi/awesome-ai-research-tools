Federated Learning & Edge AI

Speed Up Federated Learning in Heterogeneous Environments: A Dynamic Tiering Approach

Seyed Mahmoud Sajjadi Mohammadabadi, S. Zawad, F. Yan, L. Yang
IEEE Internet of Things Journal (IEEE IoT-J), 2024 Impact Factor: 9.0 30+ Citations

Heterogeneity in edge device compute and communication capacities introduces severe straggler delays in federated optimization. We introduce a dynamic tiering methodology grouping clients adaptively by throughput and latency, cutting federated training completion wall-clock time by over 80% without sacrificing model convergence or global classification accuracy.

Federated Learning & Multi-Agent

Communication-Efficient Training Workload Balancing for Decentralized Multi-Agent Learning

Seyed Mahmoud Sajjadi Mohammadabadi, L. Yang, F. Yan, J. Zhang
IEEE 44th International Conference on Distributed Computing Systems (ICDCS), 2024 Acceptance Rate: 21.9% 24+ Citations

Decentralized learning protocols suffer from severe communication overheads when agents possess unbalanced data allocations. We formulate a mathematically bounded communication-efficient workload balancer that reallocates computational stress across adjacent graph neighbors, delivering a 26% reduction in slow-agent compute burdens while guaranteeing consensus convergence.

PEFT & Efficient Fine-Tuning

SOLAR: Subspace-Oriented Lightweight Adapter Reparameterization for Scalable PEFT Compression

Seyed Mahmoud Sajjadi Mohammadabadi, X. Ma, L. Yang, F. Yan, J. Zhang
arXiv Preprint • Under Review, 2026 35% Lower Memory than LoRA Open Source

Standard Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA) scales linearly with model rank and weight matrix dimensions, incurring significant transmission costs during distributed edge adaptation. SOLAR reparameterizes low-rank projections into orthogonal latent subspaces, achieving 35% smaller memory footprint and transmission payloads across Vision Transformers (ViT), LLaMA-3, and GPT-2 without degradation on downstream benchmarks.
