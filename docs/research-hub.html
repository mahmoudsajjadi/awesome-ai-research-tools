⭐ Flagship Spotlight • PEFT & Foundation Model Compression

SOLAR: Communication-Efficient Model Adaptation via Subspace-Oriented Latent Adapter Reparametrization

Seyed Mahmoud Sajjadi Mohammadabadi, Xiaolong Ma, Lei Yang, Feng Yan, Junshan Zhang
arXiv preprint arXiv:2604.08368 • 2026 35.1% Lower Memory & Sync Payload than LoRA | 1.54x Faster Sync | Under Review ICML 2026

Standard Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA) scales linearly with model rank and weight matrix dimensions, incurring significant transmission costs during distributed edge adaptation. SOLAR reparameterizes low-rank projections into orthogonal latent subspaces, achieving 35% smaller memory footprint and transmission payloads across Vision Transformers (ViT), LLaMA-3, RoBERTa, and GPT-2 without degradation on downstream benchmarks (+0.3% GLUE score).
