# Academic Integrity, Ethics & Publisher Policies

Guidelines for ethical practices in peer-reviewed scientific publishing.

---

## 1. Publisher Policy Consensus (ACM, IEEE, Elsevier, Nature, Springer)
- **Authorship:** Computational software or automated models cannot be listed as authors on scholarly papers; authorship requires human accountability for scientific veracity.
- **Scientific Responsibility:** The human authors remain fully responsible for the accuracy of equations, mathematical proofs, experimental results, and literature citations.

---

## 2. Citation Rigor & Reproducibility
- Always verify digital object identifiers (DOIs), volumes, and pagination against primary source databases (IEEE Xplore, ACM Digital Library, Google Scholar, PubMed).
- Provide open-source code repositories, seed numbers, and benchmark logs to ensure peer reproducibility.
