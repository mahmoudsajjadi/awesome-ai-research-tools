Consensus vs. Elicit vs. SciSpace vs. Semantic Scholar: Best AI Literature Review Tools (2026)

Academic literature discovery has moved beyond simple keyword matching on Google Scholar. Modern scientific search engines use semantic embeddings, citation sentiment classification, and structured table extraction across 200M+ peer-reviewed manuscripts. This guide compares Consensus, Elicit, SciSpace, Scite.ai, and Semantic Scholar.

2. Workflow Recommendations

• Best for Empirical Research Questions ("Does X improve Y?"): Consensus.

• Best for Systematic Literature Reviews & Meta-Analysis Tables: Elicit.

• Best for Decoding Dense Math Equations & Reading PDFs: SciSpace.

• Best for Verifying Whether a Paper Has Been Refuted: Scite.ai.
